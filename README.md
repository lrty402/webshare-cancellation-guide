## What "Canceling" Actually Means at Webshare

> **Quick definition**: Canceling a Webshare plan means stopping the auto-renewal of your paid subscription. Your proxies stay live until the end of the current billing period, after which your account drops back to the free tier (10 proxies, 1 GB bandwidth) or to an inactive state, depending on your plan type.

That distinction matters. You aren't deleting your account. You aren't wiping your authentication settings, allowed IPs, or download history. You're just turning off the recurring payment.

If you genuinely want the account gone — wiped, no trace — that's a separate flow handled through account deletion, not subscription cancellation. Most users only need the second.

## What Happens After You Cancel

Cancellation isn't an instant kill switch. Here's the timeline:

- **Immediately after canceling**: Auto-renewal turns off. Your subscription is marked as "canceled" but stays active.
- **Until your billing cycle ends**: Your proxies, bandwidth, and features all kep working as normal. You paid for the month — use it.
- **After the billing cycle ends**: Your account drops to the free tier (if you were on a Proxy Server plan) or becomes inactive (residential and ISP plans). Dashboard access stays, but the paid proxies stop working.
- **After 90+ days of inactivity**: Some configurations may be archived. Nothing destructive happens automatically, but it's worth loging in occasionally if you plan to come back.

The most frequent post-cancel surprise: people forget the proxies still work for the rest of the cycle. If you cancel on the 3rd of the month and your renewal date is the 28th, you have 25 more days of full service. Use it. Run a final batch of jobs. Export anything you need.

## Commonancellation Issues and How to Fix Them

Real-world snags people hit, with the actual fixes:

**Issue: " can't find the cancel button."**
The button moves around between dashboard updates. Look under the "Plans" section, then click on your active subscription detail. The cancel option is usually at the bottom of that page, sometimes hidden behind a "Manage Subscription" link.

**Issue: "I canceled but got charged again."**
Two likely causes. First, you canceled after the renewal date had already triggered — the charge had already processed. Second, you have multiple subscriptions on the same account (e.g., separate Proxy Server and Residential subs). Cancel each one individually.

**Issue: "I want a refund but support hasn't replied."**
Webshare support typically responds within 24-48 hours during wekdays. If you've passed that window, send a follow-up referencing the original ticket number. Refund requests outside the standard policy window require human review and can take longer.

**Issue: "I canceled by mistake. Can I undo it?"**
Yes. As long as you're still inside your active billing cycle, you can reactivate the same plan from your dashboard. No new sign-up need. If the cycle has ended, you'll need to re-subscribe.

**Issue: "My proxies stopped working before the cycle ended."**
Check whether you triggered a different action — maybe a downgrade, a payment failure, or accidentally deleting your proxy list. A clean cancel doesn't kill access early. If service genuinely cts out before your billing date, that's a support ticket.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does Webshare cancellation take to process?**
The cancel itself is instant. Auto-renewal turns off the moment you confirm. Your service continues until the end of the current billing cycle, then your account drops to the free tier or inactive state.

**Q: Will I get a refund if I cancel mid-month?**
Generally no. Webshare doesn't issue prorated refunds for mid-cycle cancellations. Your service runs out the rest of the period you've paid for, then stops. First-time purchases inside the initial money-back window are the main exception.

**Q: Can I cancel and reuse the same email later?**
Yes. Canceling doesn't delete the account. You kep using the email address, return to the free tier, and re-subscribe to a paid plan whenever you want. All your saved configurations stay in place.

**Q: What's the difference between canceling and pausing?**
Canceling stops auto-renewal entirely. Pausing temporarily halts billing while keping your subscription state intact. Pause is better if you know you'll resume in 30-60 days. Cancel is cleaner if you're done indefinitely.

**Q: Do canceled accounts still get charged?**
No. Once you confirm cancellation, no further charges should occur. If you see a charge after canceling, it's almost always a charge that processed before the cancel registered, or a separate active subscription on the same account.

**Q: How do I delete my Webshare account entirely?**
Account deletion is a separate flow from subscription cancellation. Cancel your subscriptions first, then contact support requesting full account deletion. Webshare handles this manually for security reasons.

**Q: Will my proxies stop working immediately after I cancel?**
No. They kep working for the rest of your billing cycle. If you cancel on the 5th and your renewal is on the 28th, you have 23 more days of full proxy access.
